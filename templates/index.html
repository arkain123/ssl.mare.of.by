Paste the certificate (leaf), its intermediate chain, and the private key. Chain and key are optional - but without them full verification is not possible.


Check certificate installed on a live host

Enter a hostname (optionally with a port). The server will be contacted on port 443 (or the port you provide), and the certificate chain it presents will be analysed.



Convert certificates

PFX → PEM

Upload a PFX/P12 file to extract the private key, the leaf certificate and the CA chain.
Accepts binary DER (.pfx/.p12) and base64-encoded text files (as produced by keytool, ESET, certutil -encode, etc.).
